Understanding UK VAT: A Practical Guide for Businesses in 2025
VAT (Value Added Tax) is charged at 20% on most goods and services in the UK. Businesses with taxable turnover exceeding £90,000 must register for VAT with HMRC. Understanding the difference between standard rate (20%), reduced rate (5%), and zero rate (0%) is essential for accurate financial reporting and avoiding penalties.
Reference: HMRC — VAT Rates (gov.uk)

How Fintech is Reshaping International Business Payments in 2025
Fintech platforms such as Wise, Revolut, and GoCardless have significantly reduced cross-border payment costs for SMEs. Traditional wire transfers can cost 3–5% in fees, while modern fintech solutions reduce this to under 1%, enabling UK-based accountancy firms to manage global client payments with greater efficiency and transparency.
Reference: Financial Times — Fintech Section

Best Practices in Debt Recovery for UK Accountancy Practices
Effective aged debt management requires a structured escalation process — from polite reminder emails, to phone outreach, payment plan negotiation, and formal demand notices. UK businesses write off an estimated £4.4 billion in bad debt annually. Implementing a weekly aged debt review cycle can reduce outstanding balances by 30–40% within six months.
Reference: ICAEW — Credit Management Guidance
